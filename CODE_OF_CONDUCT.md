# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Welcome beginners and people with different abilities, identities and experience. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing and publishing private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation proportionately, including warnings and temporary or permanent bans. Apply rules consistently, explain decisions when safe and allow good-faith appeal. Protect reporter confidentiality.

Before public repository launch, the maintainer must add a reachable private conduct-reporting contact. Do not ask people to post sensitive reports publicly. Until then, community moderation setup is incomplete and the project is pre-release.
