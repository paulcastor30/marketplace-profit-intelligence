Know what you may actually earn from each sale. Enter your selling price and product cost, review your seller fees, and calculate estimated profit and margin for Shopee Philippines.

Expand optional costs for packaging, advertising, seller vouchers, shipping, affiliate payments and other expenses. See an auditable breakdown, test a promotion, choose a profit goal and inspect estimated break-even price, ROI, maximum ad budget and break-even ROAS.

The extension can read a displayed price on recognized Shopee Philippines product pages. If the page is ambiguous or unsupported, enter the price manually. Always check the selected variant and fee assumptions. Standard Marketplace category references are available; account eligibility and program choices require review; verified standard transaction and processing rules do not guarantee your actual settlement.

Your data stays on your device. No account, AI, analytics, cloud database or backend. Save defaults only when you choose to; export or delete them in Settings. Calculations are estimates, before income tax, tax credits, refunds and costs not entered. No automatic purchases or listing-price changes.

Keyboard controls, visible focus, larger text, readable explanations and text profit states are provided. Free and open source under GPL-3.0. Independent of Shopee; no affiliation or endorsement. Supports Shopee Philippines only.
