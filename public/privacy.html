Privacy & methodology

Effective 7 October 2026 · E-commerce Profit Intelligence 0.1.0

Your data stays on your device

The extension reads the displayed product name, price and product URL on Shopee Philippines product pages. These remain in Chrome session storage while Chrome is open. It never reads passwords, payment details, messages or unrelated browsing history.

Calculations run locally. Default costs and fee assumptions are saved in Chrome local storage only when you choose Save. No data is sent to us. No analytics, registration, cloud sync, AI, or backend is used.

Use Settings, data & about to export or delete all saved defaults. Uninstalling clears extension storage. Downloaded exports remain on your device until you delete them yourself.

What estimates include

Revenue is selling price multiplied by identical item quantity. Profit subtracts product cost, reviewed marketplace fees, seller vouchers, packaging, advertising, seller shipping, affiliate and other costs. Margin is profit divided by gross sales. Income tax, withholding tax credits, refunds and unentered business costs are excluded; profit is not the same as payout.

Transaction fee is generally 2.24%, rounded to pesos. Processing is ₱5 per order unless the first-50-orders monthly waiver applies. Standard Marketplace category references and named MDV/Live shipping/program caps are provided. Account eligibility, overrides and unresolved settlement details require your review. VAT-inclusive fees are not taxed again. Minimum and target prices use a conservative rounding allowance.

Rule source checked 7 October 2026: Shopee Philippines Terms of Service. This extension is independent of Shopee.

Accessibility

Native labeled controls, keyboard navigation, visible focus, text results and optional larger text are provided. Automated accessibility checks supplement manual tests. Full assistive-technology and real-user validation is required before public release.
